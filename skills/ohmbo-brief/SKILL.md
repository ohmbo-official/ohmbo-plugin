# Prepare an Ohmbo brief

Read [brief template](../../references/brief-template.md), [services](../../references/services.md), and [website links](../../references/website-links.md). Reply in the user's language.

1. Extract known information from the conversation and any user-provided material. Distinguish confirmed details from assumptions and missing details.
2. Choose the starting point: product idea, existing schematic/design, or fabrication and assembly files. Ask up to three important questions when helpful; mark other unknowns as to be confirmed.
3. Produce a concise, copyable brief appropriate to that starting point. Preserve firmware, enclosure, appearance and testing needs when relevant.
4. For a new product, link to the Ohmbo homepage and explain that the user can paste the brief into the project description. For an existing schematic or fabrication request, link to the contact page and explain how to submit it. Offer the pricing page when requested.

Do not silently send information, place an order, create a project, claim to have saved the brief to Ohmbo, or embed the brief in a URL. The user copies it to the website. Avoid inventing components, requirements, estimates, prices, or validation results.
